How it works

  1. 1

    Copy the link

    In Instagram, YouTube or Facebook, tap Share, then Copy link. Pasting the whole shared message works too.

  2. 2

    Pick a size

    Medium suits most phones and is easy on data. Choose Audio if you only want the sound.

  3. 3

    Get video, then Download

    The video saves to your device. Below is where to find it on each one.

Which sites work

Checked in November 2026. Only public posts can be saved.

  • YouTube and ShortsWorks

    When YouTube is busy, a backup service fetches the video, so it can take a little longer.

  • InstagramWorks

    Public reels and posts. Stories need a login, so they can't be saved.

  • FacebookWorks

    Public videos and Reels.

  • XWorks

    Posts that have a video.

  • SnapchatWorks

    Public Spotlight videos. Private snaps and stories can't be saved.

  • RedditNot available

    Reddit now only shows videos to signed-in accounts.

  • TikTokNot in India

    TikTok is blocked in India, so it can't be reached from here.

Where your video goes

  • iPhone

    Safari asks before downloading, then saves to the Files app, in Downloads. To move it to Photos, open it, tap Share, then Save Video.

  • Android

    Saves to Downloads and shows up in your Gallery. Add this page to your home screen and Clipnest appears in the Share menu.

  • Computer

    Saves to your Downloads folder.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Clipnest runs on free services, so please keep your private link within the family.

Why does it say the post is private or needs a login?

The video isn't public, or the site only shows it to signed-in people. Clipnest can only save public videos.

Why is there no sound?

Some videos are posted without sound. When the site says so, Clipnest shows "no sound" before you download.

How long can a video be?

Up to 15 minutes and 200 MB. For longer videos, choose Low.

Can I share straight from Instagram or YouTube?

On Android, yes. Open your private link in Chrome, choose Add to Home screen, and Clipnest appears when you tap Share in other apps.

What does Clipnest keep?

Nothing on the server: each file is deleted as soon as it's sent. Your recent links are remembered only on your own device.
